Step 1

Master Remote Browser

The Remote Browser is a secure online workspace environment provided during the GCE exam. Ensure you perform all lab work inside it.

Setup Instructions Checklist

ACTION 1

Go to the Link

Click the "Remote Browser" link provided in your exam instructions. Keep this exam instructions page open in a separate window if needed.

ACTION 2

Copy the Access Token/Link

Click 'Copy' on the console view to capture the secure access key token directly into your buffer.

ACTION 3

Open a New Tab

Open a completely new workspace tab (Ctrl + T), paste the copied secure link into the address bar, and hit Enter.

ACTION 4

Sign-in Automatically

Sign in automatically with the workspace user account/credentials configured specifically for your exam. DO NOT use personal credentials.

ACTION 5

Wait for Desktop & Run Lab

Let the browser interface initialize. Once fully loaded, your virtual testing interface is active and ready to perform tasks!

REMOTE BROWSER MANDATORY RULES

•

Do not leave or open other tabs outside the sandbox. Your activity is proctored.

•

Save work regularly within the native Google tool editors (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms).

•

Keep your internet connection stable. Do not download large assets during the test.

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

✕

Opening tasks or files outside the sandbox environment. This will prevent automated evaluation from tracking your work.

✕

Not checking user sign-in status. Double check that the active avatar matches your exam candidate profile.

✕

Failing to hit submit on specific Google Classroom tasks once completed.

CRITICAL COLLABORATION CHEAT CODES

Double Check Sharing Roles

Changing settings to "Anyone with link can Edit" is only for general documents. When adding specific reviewers or managers, verify roles carefully: Editor vs Viewer.

Collaborator Verification

For forms, click 'Add collaborators' to add editors. Setting general link permission doesn't make collaborators. Do not confuse Form fillers with Form editors!
